Process

From bin to buyer, in four steps

Each listing moves through the same pipeline — order matters, so we track exactly where a material is at every stage.

01

List

Post a photo, waste type, quantity, location, and pickup window.

02

Classify

AI identifies the material and recommends recycle, reuse, compost, or donate.

03

Match

Nearby recyclers, NGOs, or buyers interested in that material are surfaced automatically.

04

Exchange

Pickup is arranged, the exchange is logged, and Eco Points are awarded.
